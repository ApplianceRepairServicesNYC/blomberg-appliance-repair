Blomberg Washer Error Codes

Error Code E10 - Water Supply Problem

The E10 error indicates insufficient water entering the machine within the expected timeframe. Check that supply valves are fully open, inlet hoses aren't kinked or crushed, and inlet filter screens are clean and free of sediment. Low water pressure in your building can also trigger this code, particularly during peak usage hours. If the error persists after checking these items, the water inlet valve solenoid may require professional testing and replacement.

Error Code E20 - Drainage Issue

This code appears when the washer cannot drain properly within the allotted time. Common causes include clogged drain filters filled with lint, coins, or debris, kinked drain hoses restricting water flow, or blocked drain pumps. Access the filter behind the lower front panel to clean accumulated debris. Verify the drain hose isn't pushed too far into the standpipe, which can create a siphon effect. Persistent drainage issues may indicate a failed drain pump motor requiring replacement.

Error Code E40 - Door Lock Failure

The door lock mechanism has failed to engage or release properly, preventing cycle operation for safety reasons. Causes include faulty door lock assemblies, misaligned door strikes, worn door hinges affecting alignment, or control board communication issues. Inspect the door seal for objects preventing complete closure. The door lock assembly contains both mechanical and electrical components that must function together, and failure of either requires professional diagnosis and repair.

Error Code E90 - Communication Error

This indicates communication failure between the main control board and motor control unit or other electronic components. Power cycling the machine by unplugging for five minutes may resolve temporary glitches caused by electrical interference or voltage fluctuations. Persistent errors suggest wiring harness problems, corroded connections, or failed control boards requiring professional diagnosis with specialized equipment.

Error Code E11 - Fill Timeout Extended

Similar to E10 but indicates the fill cycle exceeded maximum time limits. This often occurs when water pressure is marginal or inlet valve flow is restricted. Check for partially closed valves or clogged inlet screens. In older buildings with galvanized pipes, sediment accumulation can significantly reduce flow rates.

Error Code E21 - Drain Pump Blockage

The drain pump is running but water level isn't decreasing, indicating a blockage in the pump impeller or check valve. Small items like coins, buttons, or hair ties commonly cause this issue. The pump filter requires regular cleaning to prevent blockages and maintain proper drainage performance.

Blomberg Dryer Error Codes

Error Code E50 - Motor Problem

The motor is not operating correctly due to overheating from blocked ventilation, mechanical overload from tangled items, or internal motor failure. Allow the dryer to cool for at least 30 minutes and retry with a smaller load. Check for items wrapped around the drum that could impede rotation. Persistent errors indicate motor bearing failure, control board communication issues, or motor winding problems requiring professional diagnosis and repair.

Error Code E60 - Heating System Fault

The dryer cannot achieve proper heating temperatures for the selected cycle. In heat pump models, this may indicate refrigerant leaks reducing system efficiency, compressor failures preventing heat generation, blocked heat exchangers reducing thermal transfer, or temperature sensor failures providing incorrect readings. This error requires professional service as heat pump systems contain sealed refrigerant circuits and specialized components.

Error Code E70 - Temperature Sensor Error

The NTC temperature sensor is reading incorrectly or has failed completely, preventing accurate temperature regulation during cycles. This affects cycle timing, heat output control, and automatic shut-off functions. Symptoms include clothes coming out damp or excessively hot regardless of cycle selection. Sensor replacement is typically required, involving access to internal components and calibration with the control system.

Error Code E80 - Airflow Restriction

Insufficient airflow detected through the drying system, often from a full lint filter blocking air passage, blocked condenser coils reducing heat exchange, or restricted internal air pathways. Clean the lint filter before every use and the condenser monthly for optimal performance. In ventless models, restricted airflow dramatically reduces efficiency and extends drying times while potentially triggering safety shutoffs.

Error Code E90 - Electronic Control Fault

The main control board has detected an internal error or communication failure with sensors and actuators. Power cycling by unplugging for five minutes may clear temporary glitches. Persistent errors suggest control board failure requiring replacement, or wiring harness problems affecting component communication.

Error Code F1 - Water Container Full

The condensate collection tank has reached capacity and must be emptied before the cycle can continue. This is normal operation for ventless dryers not connected to a drain. Empty the tank, located typically behind a lower front panel, and the cycle should resume automatically or after pressing start.

Heat pump dryers may experience compressor failures where the refrigeration cycle cannot generate heat, refrigerant leaks that reduce system capacity gradually, control board failures that prevent compressor activation, or sensor issues that incorrectly signal temperature achievement. Rattling may indicate loose panels, worn felt seals, or objects caught in the blower housing.

Dryer Won't Start

When the dryer fails to respond, check that the door is completely closed and latched, as the door switch prevents operation when open for safety. Verify power supply by checking circuit breakers and outlet voltage. Control board failures, start switch problems, or thermal fuse trips can all prevent startup. Blomberg Dishwasher Error Codes

Error Code E1 - Water Fill Timeout

The dishwasher did not fill with water within the expected timeframe, typically 2-3 minutes depending on cycle requirements. Check that water supply valves under the sink are fully open and inlet hoses aren't kinked or restricted. Inspect the inlet valve filter screen for sediment blockage that restricts flow. Low water pressure in older buildings during peak usage hours can also trigger this error. If supply seems adequate, the water inlet valve solenoid may have failed and require replacement.

Error Code E2 - Drainage Problem

Water is not draining properly after the wash or rinse cycles, leaving standing water in the tub bottom. Clean the drain filter assembly accessed through the tub bottom, removing food particles and debris that accumulate during normal operation. Check the drain hose for kinks, improper routing, or blockages. Verify the garbage disposal knockout plug was removed if the dishwasher drains through the disposal. If the disposal is clogged, drainage will be affected. Persistent issues indicate drain pump failure or control board problems.

Error Code E3 - Heating Fault

The water is not reaching proper temperature required for effective cleaning and sanitizing. This may indicate a failed heating element that cannot raise water temperature, a thermostat that incorrectly reads temperature, or control board issues that don't activate the heater. Some cycles require specific temperatures for proper operation, particularly sanitize cycles that must reach 150°F or higher. Professional diagnosis determines whether the element, thermostat, or control requires replacement.

Error Code E4 - Overflow Detection

Water level has exceeded safe limits, triggering the overflow protection system designed to prevent flooding. The float switch in the tub bottom activates when water rises too high, shutting off the inlet valve and activating the drain pump. This may indicate an inlet valve stuck open and continuously filling, a blocked drain preventing water evacuation, or float switch problems giving false readings. Inspect the float mechanism for debris preventing free movement.

Error Code E5 - Motor or Circulation Problem

The wash motor is not operating correctly, preventing water circulation through the spray arms. This may indicate a seized wash pump from debris damage, motor winding failure, or control board issues preventing motor activation. Listen for motor humming without rotation, which suggests mechanical seizure. Complete silence indicates electrical failure.

Error Code E7 - Door Latch Failure

The door latch mechanism has not engaged properly, preventing cycle operation for safety. Inspect the latch strike plate alignment and clean any buildup preventing complete closure. The door switch provides the signal that allows cycle activation, and failures prevent operation even when the door appears closed.

Blomberg Oven Error Codes

Error Code F1 - Oven Temperature Sensor

The oven temperature sensor is reading incorrectly or has failed completely, preventing accurate temperature regulation in the oven cavity. This affects cooking results and may cause significant undercooking or overcooking as the control board cannot properly modulate heating elements. The sensor resistance should follow a predictable curve as temperature changes, and deviations indicate failure requiring sensor replacement and possibly calibration.

Error Code F3 - Oven Overheating

The oven exceeded safe temperature limits, triggering protective shutdown to prevent component damage and potential fire hazard. This may indicate thermostat failure allowing continuous heating, relay contacts stuck closed preventing element shutoff, restricted ventilation causing heat buildup, or sensor problems providing incorrect temperature readings to the control board. Professional diagnosis is essential as this error indicates potentially serious safety issues.

Error Code F5 - Door Lock Error

Self-cleaning ovens display this when the door lock mechanism fails to engage for the cleaning cycle or fails to release after cleaning completes. The motorized lock must fully secure before pyrolytic cleaning begins as a safety requirement, preventing door opening during the extremely high temperatures used. The lock mechanism may be mechanically stuck, the drive motor may have failed, or position sensors may be providing incorrect feedback to the control board.

Error Code F9 - Convection Fan Fault

The convection fan is not operating properly, affecting heat circulation and cooking evenness in convection modes. The fan motor may have failed, bearings may be seized, or electrical connections may be compromised. Without proper fan operation, convection cooking produces uneven results similar to standard baking. Some ovens disable convection modes entirely when this error occurs.

Error Code F2 - Oven Too Hot at Start

The oven temperature sensor detected excessive temperature when a cycle was initiated, indicating residual heat from previous use or sensor malfunction. Allow the oven to cool completely before restarting. Persistent errors suggest sensor problems or control board issues.

Error Code F4 - Element Failure

The heating element circuit is not functioning properly, either from element burnout, loose wiring connections, or relay failure on the control board. Visual inspection of elements may reveal breaks or burn marks indicating failure location.

Error Code F9 - Door Latch Error

The self-cleaning door latch is not operating correctly, preventing cleaning cycle initiation or failing to release after cleaning completes. The motorized latch must fully secure the door before high-temperature pyrolytic cleaning begins for safety. The oven may be stuck locked preventing any use, or unable to lock for cleaning cycle. Latch motor failures, mechanical jams, or position sensor problems require professional repair to restore function while maintaining safety.

Error Code F3 - Oven Temperature Exceeded

Similar to F2 but may indicate sensor reading excessively high temperature rather than actual overheating. Verify oven is cool before assuming sensor malfunction. Persistent errors with cool oven suggest sensor failure; errors during actual overheating indicate heating control problems.

Error Code F5 - Control Board Communication Error

The main control board cannot communicate with cooktop controls, oven controls, or display panel. Wiring harness problems, loose connections from vibration or thermal cycling, or component failures prevent proper communication between system components.

Common Problems and Solutions

Oven Not Heating

Gas ovens may have igniter problems where the glow bar doesn't reach temperatures needed to open the safety gas valve, resulting in clicking without gas flow or silent failure without ignition. Gas valve failures, thermocouple issues in standing pilot systems, or gas supply problems prevent fuel delivery even with working igniters. Electric ovens may have bake or broil element burnout from normal wear or stress failures, control board relay problems preventing element activation, or sensor failures providing incorrect feedback that prevents heating activation. We diagnose and repair all oven heating components using systematic testing procedures.

Burners Not Lighting

Igniter problems are the most common cause of gas burner lighting failures. Spark igniters may be dirty with food residue, cracked ceramic, improperly positioned, or electrically failed. Hot surface igniters in some models may be weak or failed. Clogged burner ports from boiled-over food prevent proper gas-air mixing for ignition. Gas valve problems ranging from stuck closed to electrical failures prevent fuel flow regardless of igniter condition. Our technicians systematically diagnose ignition systems and restore reliable burner operation with proper component repair or replacement.

Uneven Oven Temperature

Hot spots or uneven cooking results indicate problems affecting heat distribution throughout the oven cavity. Convection fan failures stop air circulation that normally equalizes temperatures throughout the space. Sensor problems cause the control board to incorrectly regulate heating. Partial element failures in electric ovens create zones of reduced heat output. Damaged door gaskets allow heat escape creating temperature gradients near the front. Improper airflow from blocked vents affects temperature distribution. We diagnose the specific cause of uneven heating and restore consistent oven performance.

Gas Smell

If you smell gas, this is a serious safety issue requiring immediate attention. Turn off the range and gas supply if safely accessible. Ventilate the area by opening windows. Do not attempt to light burners or operate electrical switches. Evacuate if the smell is strong. Gas odors may indicate failed burner valve seals, loose connections at the gas supply, or ignition problems allowing unburned gas release. Contact us for emergency service to identify and repair gas leaks safely.

Self-Clean Not Working

Self-cleaning cycles may fail to start if the door latch doesn't properly engage, sensors detect existing error conditions, or control board prevents activation. The oven must be cool before cleaning cycles begin. Persistent failures indicate latch mechanism problems, sensor issues, or control board failures requiring professional diagnosis.
